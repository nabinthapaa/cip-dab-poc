Definitions & method

Every live figure on this console is one column of one contract view. This page is the single reference for how the numbers are made and where they come from.

Invisible Care Index — composition

Index = 0.40 × Environmental + 0.30 × PPM compliance + 0.20 × Critical work orders + 0.10 × Asset condition

Environmental is the mean of IAQ, water quality, humidity and fire-system integrity, each averaged over the 15 minutes up to its latest reading. PPM compliance is rolling 90-day completion. The critical work-order score starts at 100 and loses 20 points per open P1/P2 per 1,000 tagged assets. Asset condition is the validated survey index. Weights live in a database table (core.index_weight), so changing them after sign-off (M011) is a data change, not a release.

90–100 Nominal   80–89 Watch   <80 Attention

Known limitations of this proof of concept

All figures are synthetic, map positions are approximate and estates are identified by code only. STATIC panels are illustrative and carry the mapping item that would make them live.
